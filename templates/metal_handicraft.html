Peshawar Metal Craft — Heritage in Metal

Peshawar and the surrounding region are known for traditional metal craftsmanship. Skilled artisans work with metals such as brass and copper to create decorative and functional handcrafted products reflecting the region’s cultural heritage.

Brass & Copper Craft

Brass and copper are widely used in traditional metal craftsmanship for producing decorative and functional handcrafted articles with distinctive textures and finishes.

Handcrafted Metal Lanterns

Traditional metal lanterns combine practical construction with decorative workmanship. Perforated patterns and handcrafted details give these pieces their distinctive character.

Decorative Metalwork

Artisans create decorative metal pieces using traditional shaping, engraving and finishing techniques. These products can serve as interior décor, gifts and cultural collectibles.

Metal Frames & Decorative Pieces

Handcrafted metal frames and decorative articles combine traditional patterns with practical contemporary applications, making them suitable for homes, hospitality spaces and gifting.

Traditional Craft for Modern Markets

Traditional metalworking can be presented through contemporary product designs while preserving the character of handmade craftsmanship for domestic and international markets.
