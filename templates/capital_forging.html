Advanced Forging Capabilities

Our forging capabilities support the production of durable, high-strength components through controlled forming processes selected according to component geometry, material and application.

01 · FORGING PROCESS

Open Forging

Open forging supports large shafts, blocks, rings and heavy-duty components using controlled deformation between open dies. The process provides flexibility for specialized geometries and large-section industrial components.

02 · FORGING PROCESS

Close Forging

Close forging forms heated metal within shaped dies to achieve repeatable component geometry and controlled material flow. It is suitable for engineered components requiring strength, consistency and production repeatability.

03 · FORGING PROCESS

Drop Forging

Drop forging uses controlled impact from a forging hammer to shape heated metal within dies. The process is suited to repeatable production of strong components with defined geometry and high structural integrity.

04 · FORGING PROCESS

Press Forging

Press forging applies controlled continuous pressure to form heated metal with accurate and repeatable deformation. It supports demanding industrial components where controlled forming and dimensional consistency are important.
