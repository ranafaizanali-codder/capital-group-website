CC’s Thread Handicraft Division connects traditional hand-embroidery craftsmanship with contemporary markets, offering carefully selected thread-based handicrafts for domestic and international sourcing.

Multan Embroidery Heritage

Multan and the wider Punjab region have a long tradition of decorative hand embroidery and textile craftsmanship. Traditional techniques combine skilled needlework, patterned stitching and decorative thread to create culturally distinctive products.

Traditional Hand Embroidery

Skilled artisans use traditional hand-stitching techniques to create detailed patterns on textile surfaces. The work reflects regional craftsmanship while allowing products to be adapted for contemporary markets.

Taarkashi & Decorative Threadwork

Taarkashi is among the traditional embroidery techniques associated with regional craftsmanship. Decorative threadwork creates intricate visual patterns and adds distinctive character to handcrafted textile products.

Kacha Tanka & Pakka Taanka

Kacha Tanka and Pakka Taanka represent traditional stitching methods used in decorative embroidery. Their detailed patterns demonstrate the precision and patience involved in handmade textile craftsmanship.

Mukesh & Kamdani Work

Mukesh and Kamdani are decorative textile traditions involving fine embellishment and detailed craftsmanship. These techniques contribute to the distinctive appearance of formal and decorative handcrafted textiles.

Contemporary Craft & Global Markets

Traditional thread craftsmanship can be presented through contemporary designs and carefully selected products for domestic and international markets, while preserving the character of handmade production.
