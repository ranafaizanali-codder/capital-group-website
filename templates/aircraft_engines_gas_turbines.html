Aircraft Engines & Gas Turbines

This professional training programme develops a technical understanding of aircraft jet engines and gas turbines, with emphasis on component performance, engine characteristics, design limitations and future trends.

Course Reference MIT 16.511
Duration 5 Days
Course Date Can be offered upon a specific date request
Venue Marriott Islamabad

Course Overview

The programme examines the performance and characteristics of aircraft jet engines and industrial gas turbines through the thermodynamic and fluid-mechanical behaviour of major engine components.

It covers inlets, compressors, combustors, turbines and nozzles, together with engine configurations, material limitations and important future design considerations.

Training Objectives

  • Understand aircraft engine operating principles.
  • Analyse fundamental gas turbine performance.
  • Understand major engine component functions.
  • Evaluate thermodynamic and fluid-mechanical behaviour.
  • Understand modern turbofan configurations.
  • Recognise key engine design limitations and trade-offs.
