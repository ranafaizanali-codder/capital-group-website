Strategic Insight and Expertise for Sustainable Business Growth

Advanced Aerospace Design & Engineering Solutions

CCs supports aerospace design activities through computer-aided design, engineering analysis, aerodynamic studies, structural assessment and technical documentation. Each assignment can be scoped around its intended mission, design requirements and applicable engineering constraints.

Aircraft Engineering

Conventional Aircraft Design

CCs can support concept development and engineering studies for conventional aircraft, with attention to mission requirements, configuration, component interfaces and preliminary design considerations.

  • Aircraft configuration studies
  • Preliminary geometry and CAD modelling
  • Component and assembly design
  • Design requirements and documentation
Uncrewed Aircraft Systems

UAV Design & Development

CCs can assist with non-operational concept design and engineering analysis for uncrewed aerial vehicles (UAVs). Work can be scoped around intended payload, endurance, airframe layout and environmental operating requirements.

  • Airframe concept development
  • CAD models and assembly layouts
  • Weight and packaging considerations
  • Preliminary aerodynamic assessment
Mission-Specific Design Studies

Fighter & Military Aircraft Design Studies

CCs can provide general aerospace engineering support for aircraft configuration, mechanical components, structural concepts and system interfaces. The exact scope depends on the customer’s requirements and applicable regulations.

  • Aircraft configuration concepts
  • Mechanical component modelling
  • Structural and material considerations
  • Engineering drawings and technical records
Aerospace Components

Aerospace Modules & Subassemblies

CCs supports design studies for aerospace parts, modules and mechanical subassemblies. Design work can consider dimensional accuracy, material selection, interfaces, manufacturability and maintenance requirements.

  • Precision component modelling
  • Mechanical interfaces and assemblies
  • Material and tolerance considerations
  • Manufacturing-oriented design documentation
Aerodynamic Engineering

Aerodynamics & Computational Fluid Dynamics

Computational fluid dynamics (CFD) tools can be used in suitable projects to study airflow, pressure distribution and aerodynamic behaviour. Analysis scope and accuracy depend on the model, boundary conditions and validation approach.

  • Preliminary airflow studies
  • CFD model preparation
  • Pressure and flow-field assessment
  • Simulation results interpretation
Structural Engineering

Structural Analysis & Design Validation

Structural analysis helps evaluate how components respond to specified loads and constraints. Depending on the project scope, finite element analysis (FEA) may support preliminary assessment of stress, deformation and design-related engineering questions.

  • Finite element modelling
  • Stress and deformation assessment
  • Material and load-case considerations
  • Technical reporting and design review
