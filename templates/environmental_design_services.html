Environmental Assessment

Environmental Impact Assessment & Planning

Environmental assessment helps identify potential environmental effects associated with a proposed project. Early planning can support impact identification, mitigation planning and informed project decisions.

  • Environmental baseline studies
  • Potential impact identification
  • Mitigation planning support
  • Environmental documentation
Sustainable Engineering

Sustainable Design & Resource Efficiency

Sustainable design considers energy, water, materials and resource use throughout project planning. Engineering studies can help compare alternatives and identify opportunities to reduce avoidable resource consumption.

  • Resource efficiency studies
  • Energy-use assessment
  • Material and lifecycle considerations
  • Sustainable design alternatives
Air Quality Engineering

Air Quality & Emissions Assessment

Air quality studies examine relevant emission sources and environmental conditions. Depending on the project, modelling and available monitoring data may support the evaluation of potential air quality impacts.

  • Emission-source assessment
  • Air quality data analysis
  • Dispersion modelling support
  • Mitigation option studies
Water & Hydrology

Water Resource Management & Hydrological Studies

Water-related engineering studies can help assess drainage, runoff, water availability and potential environmental effects. Appropriate methods depend on site conditions and the quality of available data.

  • Water resource assessment
  • Runoff and drainage studies
  • Watershed modelling support
  • Water management planning
Waste & Circular Economy

Waste management planning considers waste generation, handling, treatment and disposal options. A systematic approach can help identify opportunities for waste prevention, material recovery and improved resource use.

  • Waste-stream assessment

  • Pollution prevention planning

  • Spatial planning support
