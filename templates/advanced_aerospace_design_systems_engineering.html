Advanced Aerospace Design & Systems Engineering

This professional programme introduces the complete aerospace design process, from engineering requirements and conceptual development to aircraft configuration, systems integration, computational analysis and design evaluation.

Aerospace Design

Design & Systems Engineering

Course Overview

The programme develops a systems-level understanding of aerospace design. It connects aircraft configuration, performance, aerodynamics, structures, propulsion and engineering requirements into an integrated design process. Computational methods and design evaluation are introduced to support informed engineering decisions.

Professional Learning Objectives

  • Understand the major stages of the aerospace design process.
  • Analyse engineering requirements and translate them into practical design objectives.
  • Develop conceptual aircraft configurations and evaluate their engineering characteristics.
  • Apply systems engineering principles to aerospace design.
  • Understand the role of computational analysis in modern aircraft development.
  • Evaluate design alternatives using performance, technical and operational considerations.

Key Training Areas

Aerospace Design Process

Requirements, concept generation, design decisions and development stages.

Systems Engineering

Requirements analysis, system architecture and integrated aerospace engineering solutions.

Aircraft Conceptual Design

Aircraft configuration, preliminary sizing and performance-driven design decisions.

Computational Aerospace Analysis

Computational methods for aircraft modelling, analysis and design evaluation.

Aircraft Configuration

Fuselage, wing, propulsion, empennage and landing gear configuration considerations.

Design Optimisation

Engineering optimisation approaches for improving aerospace design performance.

Aerospace Design Applications

The programme applies aerospace engineering principles to conceptual aircraft development, system-level design, configuration analysis and computational engineering. Participants develop a structured approach to solving multidisciplinary aerospace design problems.

Integrated Aerospace Design

Modern aerospace design requires different engineering disciplines to work together. This programme brings design, systems engineering, computational analysis, aircraft configuration and performance evaluation into one integrated engineering approach.

Professional Outcomes

  • Develop a structured approach to aerospace design problems.
  • Understand how aircraft subsystems contribute to the overall vehicle design.
  • Interpret engineering requirements within a systems-level design process.
  • Apply computational and analytical methods to aerospace design tasks.
  • Evaluate alternative aircraft configurations and engineering solutions.

Programme Development

The technical structure of this programme is informed by aerospace design and systems-engineering topics taught in leading university aerospace programmes, including aircraft conceptual design, systems engineering, computational aerospace methods and integrated design practice.
