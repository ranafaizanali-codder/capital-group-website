Aerospace Propulsion & Engine Systems

This professional training programme develops a system-level understanding of aerospace propulsion, covering propulsion requirements, engine performance, mission analysis and integration of propulsion systems into flight vehicles.

Course Reference MIT 16.50
Duration 5 Days
Course Date Can be offered upon a specific date request
Venue Marriott Islamabad

Course Overview

The programme examines aerospace propulsion devices as integrated engineering systems, with emphasis on functional requirements, performance, environmental limitations and propulsion selection for different flight missions.

The training covers both air-breathing and rocket propulsion concepts, together with fundamental performance relationships and practical propulsion system integration.

Training Objectives

  • Understand fundamental aerospace propulsion principles.
  • Analyse propulsion system requirements and constraints.
  • Evaluate fundamental engine performance relationships.
  • Understand air-breathing propulsion systems.
  • Understand rocket propulsion system fundamentals.
  • Integrate propulsion considerations into vehicle-level design.

5-Day Professional Training Programme

A structured programme covering the major engineering principles required to understand, analyse and integrate aerospace propulsion systems.

Day 1 — Propulsion Systems Fundamentals

  • Fundamentals of aerospace propulsion
  • Propulsion system functional requirements
  • Thrust and propulsion performance concepts
  • Energy conversion in propulsion systems
  • Propulsion system design constraints
  • Engineering and environmental considerations

Day 2 — Air-Breathing Propulsion

  • Fundamentals of air-breathing engines
  • Turbojet and turbofan propulsion concepts
  • Inlet and compressor functions
  • Combustion and turbine processes
  • Nozzle and exhaust flow concepts
  • Air-breathing propulsion performance

Day 3 — Rocket Propulsion Fundamentals

  • Fundamentals of rocket propulsion
  • Rocket engine operating principles
  • Propellant and energy considerations
  • Thrust generation mechanisms
  • Nozzle performance fundamentals
  • Launch and space mission applications

Day 4 — Mission Analysis & Performance

  • Propulsion mission requirements
  • Engine performance relationships
  • Thrust and efficiency considerations
  • Propulsion system sizing concepts
  • Performance trade-offs
  • Mission-level propulsion analysis

Day 5 — Propulsion System Integration

  • Integration of propulsion into flight vehicles
  • Vehicle-level propulsion requirements
  • Engine selection considerations
  • Propulsion and aircraft performance interaction
  • Engineering design case studies
  • Final technical review and discussion

Aerospace Propulsion in Practice

Apply propulsion engineering principles to real-world aerospace vehicle requirements, performance analysis and integrated propulsion system design.
