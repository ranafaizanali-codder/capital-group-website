Circuit Engineering

Electronic Circuit Design & Schematic Capture

Electronic circuit design defines how components connect and work together to meet functional requirements. Schematic tools help engineers document circuit structure and prepare designs for review and simulation.

  • Electronic schematic development
  • Component selection studies
  • Circuit documentation
  • Design review and refinement
Printed Circuit Boards

PCB Layout & Board Design

Printed circuit board design translates an electronic schematic into a physical board layout. Design considerations may include component placement, routing, board dimensions and manufacturing rules.

  • PCB component placement
  • Track routing and layout
  • Board stack-up considerations
  • Design-rule checking
Electronic Simulation

Circuit Simulation & Performance Analysis

Circuit simulation can help examine expected behaviour before hardware is produced. Depending on the model, engineers may study voltage, current, timing and responses to defined operating conditions.

  • Analogue circuit simulation
  • Digital logic verification
  • Transient and frequency analysis
  • Simulation result interpretation
Embedded Electronics

Embedded Systems & Hardware Integration

Embedded electronics combine hardware components with control and processing functions. Design activities may include board-level interfaces, peripheral connectivity and system integration planning.

  • Microcontroller-based circuit concepts
  • Sensor and interface connections
  • Hardware integration planning
  • Technical design documentation
Electronic Systems

Power, Signal & Electronic System Design

Electronic systems may require separate consideration of power delivery, signal integrity, component compatibility and electrical operating conditions. These factors can be reviewed during design development.

  • Power supply circuit concepts
  • Signal interface planning
  • Component compatibility reviews
  • Electrical design documentation
Verification & Documentation

Design Verification & Engineering Documentation

Design verification helps identify issues before a design moves to manufacturing or integration. The appropriate checks depend on project requirements, simulation assumptions and available test information.

  • Electrical design checks
  • Simulation-based review
  • PCB design-rule verification
  • Technical reports and design records
