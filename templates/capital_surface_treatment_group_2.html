Surface Treatment Capabilities · 13–24

Our second range covers protective coatings, vapour deposition, thermal processing and specialized metallurgical treatments for demanding industrial and engineering applications.

13 · BLACKENING

Blackening

Blackening produces a controlled dark oxide finish on suitable metal components. It provides surface protection and controlled appearance while supporting dimensional stability for precision parts.

14 · GALVANIZING

Galvanizing

Galvanizing applies a protective zinc layer to suitable steel and iron components. The coating provides a practical barrier against corrosion for industrial structures and engineered parts.

15 · CVD

CVD — Chemical Vapour Deposition

Chemical Vapour Deposition forms engineered coatings through controlled chemical reactions in a vapour environment. It is used where improved wear, thermal and surface performance is required.

16 · PVD

PVD — Physical Vapour Deposition

Physical Vapour Deposition creates thin engineered coatings through controlled vacuum processes. It can improve surface hardness, wear resistance and functional performance of selected components.

17 · PLASTIC POWDER COATING

Plastic Powder Coating

Powder coating applies a durable polymer-based finish to suitable metal surfaces. It provides corrosion protection, surface durability and a controlled decorative or functional finish.

18 · THERMAL BARRIER COATING

Thermal Barrier Coating (TBC)

Thermal Barrier Coatings provide thermal protection for selected high-temperature components. They help reduce heat transfer and support component performance in demanding thermal environments.

19 · OPEN FURNACE HEAT TREATMENT

Open Furnace Heat Treatment

Open furnace heat treatment modifies the mechanical properties and metallurgical structure of suitable components through controlled heating, soaking and cooling cycles.

20 · VACUUM FURNACE HEAT TREATMENT

Vacuum Furnace Heat Treatment

Vacuum furnace processing provides controlled heat treatment in a low-pressure environment, helping minimize oxidation and contamination during thermal processing of precision components.

21 · CASE HARDENING

Case Hardening

Case hardening develops a hard wear-resistant surface while retaining a comparatively tougher core. It is commonly used for components exposed to repeated contact, friction and mechanical loading.

22 · ANNEALING

Annealing

Annealing uses controlled heating and cooling to modify material structure and relieve internal stresses. It can improve machinability, ductility and dimensional stability.

23 · NITRIDING

Nitriding

Nitriding introduces nitrogen into the surface of suitable alloys to develop a hard, wear-resistant layer. The process supports components requiring enhanced surface durability and fatigue performance.

24 · PASSIVATION

Passivation

Passivation improves the corrosion resistance of suitable stainless steel and other compatible components by removing surface contaminants and supporting formation of a stable protective surface.
