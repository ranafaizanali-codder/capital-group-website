Metal Additive Manufacturing

Complex Metal Components from Digital Designs

Metal additive manufacturing produces precision components directly from digital engineering models, reducing dependence on conventional tooling.

The process supports prototypes, development parts, tooling and selected low-volume production applications.

Process, material and post-processing requirements are considered according to the intended component application.

Metal Printing Technology

Selective Laser Melting

Selective Laser Melting builds metal components layer by layer by selectively melting metal powder using focused laser energy.

The process enables complex geometries, fine features and consolidated component designs.

Build parameters and material selection are matched to component requirements and engineering objectives.
