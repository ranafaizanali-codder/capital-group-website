CC’s Stone Handicraft Division connects the traditional stone-carving skills of Haripur and the Hazara region with contemporary domestic and international markets.

Haripur Stonecraft — Heritage in Stone

Haripur is associated with a distinctive tradition of stone carving, including work in locally available black stone and other natural materials. Skilled artisans shape, engrave and finish stone into decorative and functional pieces through detailed hand craftsmanship.

Traditional Craft

Hand-Carved Stonework

Haripur's stone-carving tradition uses chisels, hammers and careful finishing to transform raw stone into detailed handcrafted forms.

Natural Materials

Black Stone & Natural Stone

Local stone materials provide the foundation for handcrafted decoration, carving and architectural pieces with distinctive natural textures.

Signature Detail

Floral & Geometric Engraving

Artisans create floral, geometric and calligraphic details with controlled hand engraving, giving each piece its own character and visual depth.

Crafted Collections

Decorative & Functional Pieces

Stone craftsmanship can be developed into trays, vessels, decorative panels, architectural accents and distinctive handcrafted objects for modern spaces.
