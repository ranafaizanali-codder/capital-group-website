Surface Treatment Techniques

Precision Manufacturing Solutions par Excellence

CECs provides specialized surface treatment solutions for components manufactured from ferrous and non-ferrous alloys, plastics and other engineering materials.

Surface Treatment Capabilities · 01–12

Our first range of surface treatment capabilities supports surface preparation, cleaning, finishing, corrosion protection, wear resistance and specialized engineering requirements.

01 · SAND BLASTING

Sand Blasting

Sand blasting provides controlled surface preparation by removing contaminants, oxidation and unwanted surface layers. It prepares components for coating, painting, plating and other finishing processes.

02 · SHOT PEENING

Shot Peening

Shot peening improves fatigue resistance by introducing controlled compressive stresses into component surfaces. It is suitable for demanding mechanical and engineering applications.

03 · ELECTROLESS POLISHING

Electroless Polishing

Electroless polishing provides controlled chemical surface finishing for selected metal components. The process improves surface smoothness, cleanliness and functional finishing characteristics.

04 · ULTRASONIC CLEANING

Ultrasonic Cleaning

Ultrasonic cleaning uses high-frequency sound energy to remove contaminants from component surfaces and difficult-to-reach areas, supporting precision cleaning requirements.

05 · CONVENTIONAL GOLD PLATING

Conventional Gold Plating

Conventional gold plating provides a controlled gold finish for components requiring electrical conductivity, corrosion resistance and specialized surface protection in demanding applications.

06 · VACUUM GOLD PLATING

Vacuum Gold Plating

Vacuum gold plating supports controlled deposition of gold-based protective finishes under vacuum conditions. It can provide specialized appearance, protection and functional surface characteristics.

07 · NICKEL CADMIUM PLATING

Nickel Cadmium Plating

Nickel cadmium plating is used for specialized corrosion protection and engineered surface performance. It supports selected components exposed to demanding industrial and aerospace environments.

08 · NICKEL PLATING

Nickel Plating

Nickel plating provides a durable metallic surface finish with improved corrosion resistance, wear protection and functional performance for selected engineering components.

09 · CHROME PLATING

Chrome Plating

Chrome plating provides a hard and protective surface finish for components requiring improved wear resistance, corrosion protection and controlled surface appearance.

10 · TIN PLATING

Tin Plating

Tin plating provides a functional metallic coating for selected electrical and engineering components. It supports corrosion protection, solderability and controlled surface performance.

11 · SILVER COATING

Silver Coating

Silver coating provides a highly conductive metallic surface for selected engineering and electrical applications where controlled conductivity and surface performance are required.

12 · ANODIZING & RED ANODIZING

Anodizing & Red Anodizing

Anodizing creates a controlled protective oxide layer on suitable metal components, improving corrosion resistance and surface durability while allowing specialized finishes and colours.
