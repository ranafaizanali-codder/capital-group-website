Mechanical Design & Engineering Solutions

CCs supports mechanical engineering projects through computer-aided design, 3D modelling, technical drawings, simulation and design analysis. Project scope can be tailored to the required application, available technical information and engineering constraints.

Computer-Aided Design

3D Mechanical Design & CAD Modelling

CCs can support the development of 3D component models and mechanical assemblies, translating engineering requirements into clear digital designs for review and further development.

  • 3D part and assembly modelling
  • Parametric design workflows
  • Design revisions and configurations
  • CAD model preparation
Machine Engineering

Machine & Equipment Design

Mechanical equipment design requires careful consideration of operating conditions, component interactions, service access and functional requirements. Design studies can address these factors at the appropriate project stage.

  • Machine component concepts
  • Mechanical assembly layouts
  • Equipment arrangement studies
  • Maintenance and access considerations
Product Development

Product Design & Development

CCs can assist with product concept development and mechanical design refinement, considering usability, dimensions, material choices, assembly requirements and manufacturing feasibility.

  • Concept-to-CAD development
  • Product geometry and dimensions
  • Material and component selection studies
  • Design refinement and documentation
Engineering Analysis

Structural Analysis & Finite Element Modelling

Finite element analysis (FEA) can help assess how a model responds to specified loads, constraints and material properties. Results depend on the quality of the model, assumptions, boundary conditions and validation.

  • Finite element model preparation
  • Stress and deformation studies
  • Load-case assessment
  • Engineering results interpretation
Thermal & Fluid Engineering

Thermal Analysis & Computational Fluid Dynamics

Depending on the application, thermal analysis and CFD may be used to study heat transfer, airflow, fluid movement and temperature distribution in mechanical systems.

  • Preliminary thermal studies
  • Fluid-flow simulation preparation
  • Heat-transfer assessment
  • Simulation-based design review
Design for Manufacturing

Manufacturing Design & Technical Drawings

Mechanical designs must communicate dimensions, interfaces and manufacturing requirements clearly. Design-for-manufacturing considerations can help identify practical production and assembly issues early in the design process.

  • Engineering drawings
  • Dimensional and tolerance information
  • Assembly and interface details
  • Manufacturing feasibility reviews
