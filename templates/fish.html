Fish Division

EXPORT, IMPORT AND GLOBAL SOURCING / SUPPLY CHAIN SOLUTIONS.

CTC’s Fish Division provides professional sourcing and international trade solutions for chilled and frozen fish products, connecting reliable fisheries, processors and suppliers with distributors, food businesses and global markets.

Pakistan’s Leading Fish Species

CTC’s Fish Division focuses on professionally sourced Pakistani freshwater and marine fish products, supporting buyers through organized procurement, quality-focused handling, quantity planning, chilled and frozen logistics, export coordination and international market access.

01

Rohu

Labeo rohita

Rohu is one of Pakistan’s major freshwater fish products. CTC supports professionally sourced Rohu requirements through organized procurement, quality-focused handling, quantity planning and chilled or frozen supply coordination for commercial buyers.

02

Indian Oil Sardine

Sardinella longiceps

Indian Oil Sardine is an important marine fish associated with Pakistan’s seafood sector. CTC supports commercial sourcing through coordinated procurement, quantity planning, quality-focused handling and international supply arrangements.

03

Indian Mackerel

Rastrelliger kanagurta

Indian Mackerel is a commercially important marine fish traded across regional and international seafood markets. CTC supports sourcing, quantity coordination, chilled and frozen requirements and professional export supply arrangements.

04

Mrigal Carp

Cirrhinus mrigala

Mrigal is a recognized freshwater carp species within Pakistan’s aquaculture sector. CTC supports commercial sourcing with attention to product quality, planned quantities, cold-chain requirements and dependable supply.

05

Grass Carp

Ctenopharyngodon idella

Grass Carp is an established freshwater aquaculture species in Pakistan. CTC supports commercial sourcing and supply coordination with a focus on consistent quality, planned quantities, chilled or frozen handling and reliable market distribution.

06

Silver Carp

Hypophthalmichthys molitrix

Silver Carp is an important freshwater species within Pakistan’s fish production sector. CTC supports coordinated sourcing and international trade requirements through dependable quantities, quality-focused handling and organized chilled or frozen supply.
