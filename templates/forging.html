Forging Processes & Capabilities

Forging shapes metal under compressive forces to produce components with dependable mechanical properties. Our process selection focuses on component geometry, material requirements, production volume and intended application.

PROCESS 01

Free Hammer Forging

Free hammer forging shapes heated metal through repeated hammer blows, allowing flexible production of large or specialized components. It is suitable for custom parts where strength, material control and adaptable geometry are important.

PROCESS 02

Die Forging

Die forging forms metal within shaped dies to achieve repeatable component geometry and consistent production. It is well suited to engineered parts requiring controlled dimensions, repeatability and efficient manufacturing.

Engineering Applications

Forged components can support mechanical equipment, industrial machinery, shafts, connecting elements and other demanding applications. The appropriate forging method depends on the selected alloy, design, required tolerances and production needs.
