Asset Integrity Management for Marine Terminal Storage Tanks

CC’s professional marine engineering training develops practical expertise in asset integrity, inspection, reliability, corrosion management and lifecycle performance of marine terminal storage tanks.

OG-S 110A - Asset Integrity Management for Marine Terminal Storage Tanks

Course Reference OG-S 110A
Duration 5 Days
Course Date Can be offered upon a specific date request
Venue Marriott Islamabad

Course Description

The assets of a company should be kept in good condition working effectively and efficiently to ensure that the final product reaches the end user in time. The safety and reliability of operations and technical integrity of assets helps the company achieve target objectives. Therefore, it is necessary that asset integrity management be performed correctly so that business processes, systems, tools, competence and resources run correctly throughout the asset lifecycle.

In this course, participants will learn about the details of asset integrity management. They will also learn how to perform inspection, calibration, Reliability, corrosion management monitoring and auditing for marine terminal storage tanks.

Course Objectives

  • Understanding the basic principles of asset integrity management.
  • Applying these principles to marine terminal storage tanks.
  • Maximizing productivity of facilities.
  • Ensuring reliability and safety of equipment.
  • Reduce risks and meeting all relevant regulations.

Who should Attend?

  • Maintenance staff
  • Engineers
  • Inspectors
  • Auditors

Course Syllabus

Day 1

  • Asset integrity management
  • Development and optimization of maintenance, inspection and testing (MIT) plans
  • Work processes
  • Condition assessment and monitoring
  • Analysis of plant performance as part of Root Cause assessment
  • Technical inspections
  • Technical integrity analysis

Day 2

  • Life Cycle Asset Management (LCAM)
  • Barrier management
  • Safety risk management
  • Offshore hazard and risk assessment

Day 3

  • Dimensional control
  • Smart asset optimization
  • Surveying and mapping

Day 4

  • Storage Tank & Piping inspection (API 653) (API 570) (API 571)

  • Corrosion
  • Erosion
  • Fatigue
  • Cracking

Day 5

  • Visual Testing (VT)
  • Ultrasonic Thickness Testing (UTT)
  • Penetrant Testing (PT)
  • Radiographic Testing (RT)
  • Magnetic Particle Testing (MT)
  • Bubble Leak Testing (BLT)
  • Corrosion Under Insulation (CUI)
