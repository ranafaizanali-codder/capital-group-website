Fabrication Division

Precision Manufacturing Solutions par Excellence

CCs Fabrication Division is versatile in providing flexible fabrication services from medium to large-scale industrial, commercial and specialized requirements, structures and vessels.

Advanced Fabrication Capabilities

Our fabrication capabilities combine precision cutting, welding and forming processes to support engineered structures, components and industrial assemblies.

01 · FABRICATION PROCESS

Laser Cutting

Precision laser cutting produces accurate profiles and complex geometries from sheet and plate materials. It supports clean edges, repeatable dimensions and efficient preparation of fabricated components.

02 · FABRICATION PROCESS

Welding

Welding joins fabricated metal components into durable structures, assemblies and engineered products. Process selection is matched to material, geometry, joint requirements and application conditions.

03 · FABRICATION PROCESS

Bending

CNC bending forms sheet and plate materials into accurate angles and profiles. Controlled tooling and programmed forming sequences support repeatable dimensions for industrial components and structures.

04 · FABRICATION PROCESS

Folding

Folding provides controlled forming of sheet metal into precise channels, panels, covers and structural profiles. It supports consistent geometry and efficient production of fabricated assemblies.
