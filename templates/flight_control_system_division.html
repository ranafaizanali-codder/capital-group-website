FLIGHT CONTROL SYSTEM DIVISION

Precision Manufacturing Solutions par Excellence

CECs Flight Control System Division specializes in precision-engineered flight control components, actuation systems and avionics hardware for advanced aerospace applications, supporting accurate aircraft control, dependable system operation and modern fly-by-wire technologies.

OUR CAPABILITIES

Aerospace Flight Control Systems

CCs provides precision-engineered flight control components and integrated technologies covering flight computers, actuators, control surfaces, hydraulic systems and electronic control hardware.

01

Flight Control Computers

High-reliability flight control computers process aircraft inputs and control commands to support accurate and dependable operation of primary and secondary flight-control functions.

02

Control Surface Actuators

Precision actuator assemblies convert electrical, hydraulic or mechanical commands into controlled movement of aircraft flight surfaces for accurate and responsive aerodynamic control.

03

Ailerons & Flaps

Engineered wing control surfaces support aircraft roll control and lift management, with precise actuation and robust mechanical interfaces for demanding flight conditions.

04

Elevators & Rudders

Precision control-surface assemblies provide aircraft pitch and yaw control through reliable mechanical interfaces, hinges and actuation components.

05

Servo & Hydraulic Systems

Servo and hydraulic actuation hardware delivers controlled force and movement to flight surfaces, supporting accurate response and reliable aerospace operation.

06

Fly-by-Wire Electronics

Advanced electronic control hardware connects flight-control computers, sensors and actuators, enabling precise command processing and modern digital aircraft control architectures.
