Advanced Avionics Engineering

Avionics System Engineering — Crash Course

CC’s professional avionics engineering training develops practical understanding of communication, electrical power, instrumentation, recording, navigation, flight-control and aircraft safety systems.

Course Reference ATC 150
Duration 5 Days
Course Date Can be offered upon a specific date request
Venue Marriott Islamabad

Course Description

Avionics systems include the equipment for communication, control, monitoring, navigation, weather and collision-avoidance, including the autopilot. This course provides theoretical knowledge training to participants having experience on multi-engine jet aircraft to perform maintenance, as well as participants from Quality and Planning Departments.

Course Objectives

  • Explain the basic principles underlying the key or core avionic systems in modern civil and military aircraft.
  • Analyze the system mathematically.
  • Show the very wide range of disciplines involved in avionic systems.
  • Meet the needs of engineers at all levels working in particular areas of avionics.

Who Should Attend?

  • Managers of Civil Aviation Authorities
  • Airport Regulators
  • Air Navigation Service Providers

Five-Day Training Programme

Day 1 — Communication Systems

  • Communication systems
  • HF, VHF and SATCOM
  • Interphone and voice recorder
  • Aural warning and audio integrating systems
  • Static discharger

Day 2 — Electrical Power Systems

  • AC power sources
  • AC power distribution
  • AC standby and essential buses
  • DC power sources
  • Aircraft battery systems

Day 3 — Instrumentation & Recording

  • Demonstration of EFIS, ECAM and EICAS
  • Primary Flight Display (PFD)
  • Navigation Display (ND)
  • Warning and system status pages
  • Failure types and system indications

Day 4 — Navigation Systems

  • ADIRU
  • Pitot-static system
  • Weather radar
  • TCAS
  • EGPWS
  • VOR and DME
  • Radio Altimeter (RA)
  • ADF, Marker and ILS

Day 5 — Lighting, Fire Protection & Auto Flight

  • External aircraft lights
  • Internal lights
  • Emergency lighting
  • Fire detection
  • Smoke detection
  • Autopilot
  • Autothrust
  • Flight director
  • Damper systems
  • Yaw damper
