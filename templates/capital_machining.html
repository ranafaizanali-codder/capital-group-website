Subtractive Manufacturing Division

Precision Manufacturing Solutions par Excellence

CECs Machining Division is among the strongest subtractive manufacturing facilities, with a diverse range of subtractive machine tools with the undermentioned capabilities.

CNC Turning

CNC Lathe Machines

CNC turning is carried out using state-of-the-art CNC lathe machines from high-quality brands including Hitachi-Seiki, Maho-Dacle and Bridgeport.

We can perform horizontal turning from 10 mm diameter with lengths up to 10 m, while vertical turning supports diameters up to 15 m.

Precision Milling

Milling Machines and Machining Centers

Milling is performed on ferrous and non-ferrous alloys using advanced milling machines and machining centers from 3-axis to 5-axis configurations.

CNC rotary tables provide increased capability for complex parts, with moving-column milling capacity for components up to 14 m in length.

Gear Manufacturing

Hobbing Machines

Gear hobbing provides precision tooth generation for cylindrical gears and other engineered gear components.

The process supports accurate gear profiles for industrial machinery, transmission and power-transfer applications.

Heavy-Duty Machining

Planer Machines

Planer machining provides controlled material removal across large workpieces requiring accurate flat and linear surfaces.

It is suitable for heavy industrial components, machine structures and large engineered parts.

Precision Shaping

Shaper Machines

Shaper machines provide controlled reciprocating cutting for slots, profiles, flat surfaces and specialized component features.

The process supports precision work across a range of industrial engineering components.

Sheet Metal Processing

Nibbling Machines

CNC nibbling enables controlled cutting of sheet-metal profiles, openings and complex contours with repeatable accuracy.

It supports efficient production of fabricated panels, enclosures and application-specific sheet-metal components.

Precision Finishing

Honing Machines

Precision honing improves bore geometry, surface finish and dimensional accuracy for critical cylindrical components.

The process is suitable for demanding components where controlled internal surface quality is required.

Advanced Surface Processing

Gouging and Multifunctional Grinding Machines

Advanced grinding and gouging capabilities support precision material removal, surface correction and controlled finishing of engineered components.

These processes provide enhanced dimensional control and surface quality for demanding manufacturing applications.

Comprehensive Surface Treatment Range

Application-focused surface treatment and finishing solutions for engineered components.
