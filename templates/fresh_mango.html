Pakistan Mango Collection

Premium Mango Varieties

From the orchards of Sindh and Punjab to international markets, our mango portfolio covers established Pakistani varieties selected for flavour, appearance, quality and commercial supply requirements.

Variety 01

Sindhri Mango

A large golden-yellow Pakistani mango known for its sweet aromatic flesh, attractive appearance and strong commercial importance.

Quality Large fruit with sweet, aromatic flesh
Yield Approx. 11 tons/acre potential
Availability Mainly June to early July
Freshness Best maintained through suitable cold-chain handling

Growing region: Mirpur Khas, Tando Allahyar and surrounding mango-producing areas of Sindh.

Variety 02

Chaunsa Mango

A celebrated Pakistani mango valued for intense sweetness, rich aroma, juicy flesh and strong demand in fresh markets.

Quality Richly sweet, aromatic and smooth flesh
Yield Approx. 13 tons/acre potential
Availability Mainly July to early August
Freshness Controlled temperature supports storage quality

Growing region: Multan, South Punjab, Rahim Yar Khan and adjoining mango-producing areas.

Variety 03

Anwar Ratol

A smaller premium mango recognised for powerful aroma, intense sweetness and distinctive delicate flavour.

Quality Highly aromatic with sweet delicate flesh
Yield Approx. 9 tons/acre potential
Availability Mainly July to August
Freshness Careful handling required after harvest

Growing region: Punjab, particularly established mango-growing areas around Multan.

Variety 04

Dussehri Mango

An elongated mango appreciated for its fragrant sweet flesh, smooth texture and established place in Pakistan's mango belt.

Quality Sweet, aromatic and smooth-textured flesh
Yield Approx. 12.8 tons/acre potential
Availability Mainly June to early July
Freshness Suitable postharvest handling recommended

Growing region: Major mango-producing districts of Punjab.

Variety 05

Langra Mango

A distinctive green-skinned mango with aromatic sweet-tangy flesh and recognised commercial importance.

Quality Aromatic, smooth and fibre-free yellow flesh
Yield Approx. 10 tons/acre potential
Availability Mainly late June to early July
Freshness Controlled handling helps preserve quality

Growing region: Major mango-producing districts of Punjab, including the Multan region.

Variety 06

Nawabpuri Mango

A late-season Chaunsa type associated with the premium White Chaunsa/Nawabpuri category in Pakistan's mango trade.

Quality Sweet, aromatic and premium smooth flesh
Market Premium fresh-fruit and export segment
Availability Late season, mainly July–August
Freshness Requires careful grading, packing and temperature control

Growing region: Major mango-producing areas of Punjab, particularly the Multan belt.

Variety 07

Chunab Gold Mango

A premium golden mango presentation selected for attractive appearance, sweetness and high-value fresh-fruit positioning.

Quality Golden appearance with sweet aromatic profile
Market Premium domestic and fresh-fruit markets
Availability Seasonal availability varies by orchard
Freshness Careful grading and temperature management

Supply approach: Availability and commercial specifications are confirmed according to seasonal orchard production and buyer requirements.
