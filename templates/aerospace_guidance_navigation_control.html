Advanced Aerospace Guidance, Navigation & Control (GNC)

CC’s professional aerospace engineering training develops practical expertise in aircraft and spacecraft guidance, navigation, dynamics, control systems and modern aerospace control architectures.

Course Reference 93853
Duration 5 Days
Course Date Can be offered upon a specific date request
Venue Marriott Islamabad

Course Description

This professional training focuses on the principles and practical application of guidance and control for aircraft and spacecraft, including aerospace vehicle dynamics, control architectures, autopilot systems and modern guidance and navigation techniques.

Course Objectives

  • Understand aerospace vehicle dynamics and control principles.
  • Develop knowledge of aircraft and spacecraft guidance systems.
  • Understand navigation and flight-control architectures.
  • Apply control techniques to aerospace vehicle systems.
  • Develop practical understanding of autopilot applications.

Who Should Attend

  • Aerospace engineers
  • Avionics engineers
  • Flight-control engineers
  • Systems engineers
  • Aircraft and spacecraft engineering professionals

Five-Day Training Programme

Day 1 — Aerospace Dynamics & Control Fundamentals

  • Aircraft and spacecraft dynamics
  • Equations of motion
  • Control-system fundamentals
  • Aerospace control architectures

Day 2 — Guidance & Navigation

  • Guidance principles
  • Navigation systems
  • Trajectory and path guidance
  • Integrated guidance and navigation concepts

Day 3 — Aerospace Control Systems

  • Feedback control
  • State-space control
  • Aircraft flight-control systems
  • Spacecraft attitude control

Day 4 — Advanced Control Applications

  • Robust control concepts
  • Adaptive control concepts
  • Autopilot systems
  • Modern aerospace control applications

Day 5 — Integrated GNC Systems

  • Integrated guidance, navigation and control
  • Aircraft and spacecraft applications
  • Control-system performance assessment
  • Practical aerospace system case studies
