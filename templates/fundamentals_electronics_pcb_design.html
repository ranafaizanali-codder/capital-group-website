Professional Training Program

EEE 143 - Fundamentals of Electronics and PCB Design: From Basics to Lab Practice (10 Days)

This intensive 10-day training program offers a comprehensive foundation in electrical and electronic engineering concepts, starting from measurement principles and electronic theory through to practical applications in PCB design and troubleshooting.

Participants will gain hands-on experience in using professional design tools, performing lab diagnostics, and applying safe maintenance techniques on real-world circuit boards. Ideal for beginners or early-career professionals, the course bridges theory and practice to build strong technical competence in modern electronics.

Learning Outcomes

Course Objectives

  • Understand and apply fundamental measurement principles and SI units.
  • Identify and describe passive and active electronic components and their functions.
  • Analyze and build both analog and digital circuits using standard laws and methods.
  • Interpret and create circuit diagrams, including DC and AC configurations.
  • Work with binary logic, ASCII codes, and microcontrollers for digital circuit design.
  • Use professional schematic and PCB design tools to develop electronic projects.
  • Implement best practices in soldering, testing, and troubleshooting of electronic components.
  • Safely operate lab equipment including multimeters, oscilloscopes, and rework stations.
Target Participants

Who Should Attend?

  • Technicians
  • Engineers
  • Staff in defense, telecom, automotive, or industrial sectors requiring basic electronics knowledge.
Ten-Day Professional Program

Day 7

Day 8

Day 9

Day 10
