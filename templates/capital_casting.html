Casting Division

Precision Manufacturing Solutions par Excellence

CECs Casting Division offers multi-casting facilities according to industry and custom-designed product requirements. We have the following casting facilities ready to provide solutions for standard and special casting requirements.

Casting Capability

Sand Casting

We offer ferrous and non-ferrous castings from small and medium to large-size components for standard and specialized applications. Our 3D sand-casting pattern-making capability reduces development time while improving production economy and flexibility.

High-Pressure Casting

Pressure Die Casting

Pressure die casting delivers repeatable, high-volume production of complex non-ferrous components with controlled dimensional accuracy, surface quality and cycle efficiency.

Rotational Casting

Centrifugal Casting

Centrifugal casting supports the production of cylindrical and rotational components by using controlled centrifugal force to achieve dense, consistent metal structures and reliable geometry.

Precision Casting

Investment Casting

Investment casting enables complex, thin-walled and precision components through advanced wax pattern and ceramic-shell processes, minimizing machining requirements and supporting demanding engineering applications.
