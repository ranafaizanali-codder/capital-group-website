Flight Vehicle Engineering & Design

This professional training programme develops a system-level understanding of flight vehicle engineering, covering vehicle requirements, configuration, performance, stability considerations and integrated design decisions.

Course Reference MIT 16.82
Duration 5 Days
Course Date Can be offered against a specific date request
Venue Marriott Islamabad

Course Overview

The programme examines the engineering process used to develop atmospheric flight vehicles against defined performance and operational requirements.

Participants explore configuration development, aerodynamic considerations, performance analysis, stability requirements and engineering trade-offs involved in integrated vehicle design.

Training Objectives

  • Understand the flight vehicle engineering design process.
  • Define vehicle-level performance requirements.
  • Evaluate aircraft configuration alternatives.
  • Understand aerodynamic and performance interactions.
  • Consider stability and control requirements.
  • Evaluate engineering design trade-offs.

5-Day Professional Training Programme

A structured programme covering the engineering principles and design decisions required to develop an integrated flight vehicle.

Day 1 — Flight Vehicle Requirements

  • Flight vehicle engineering fundamentals
  • Mission and operational requirements
  • Performance requirements
  • Vehicle sizing considerations
  • Design constraints and objectives
  • Engineering requirement definition

Day 2 — Vehicle Configuration Design

  • Flight vehicle configuration concepts
  • Fuselage and wing arrangement
  • Configuration trade-offs
  • Mass and balance considerations
  • Propulsion integration
  • Vehicle layout development

Day 3 — Aerodynamics & Performance

  • Aerodynamic configuration considerations
  • Lift and drag characteristics
  • Aircraft performance fundamentals
  • Range and endurance considerations
  • Take-off and landing performance
  • Performance trade-off analysis

Day 4 — Stability & Integrated Design

  • Flight vehicle stability requirements
  • Vehicle dynamic considerations
  • Control requirements
  • Structural and propulsion integration
  • System-level design trade-offs
  • Integrated engineering analysis

Day 5 — Vehicle Design & Engineering Review

  • Integrated flight vehicle design
  • Design optimization considerations
  • Performance verification
  • Engineering case studies
  • Design review methodology
  • Final technical discussion and review
