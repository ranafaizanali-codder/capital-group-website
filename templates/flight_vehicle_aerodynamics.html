Course Overview

The programme provides professional-level training in aerodynamic flow modelling, airfoil and lifting-surface theory, boundary-layer behaviour, compressible flow and aerodynamic force analysis. Participants also examine experimental techniques and aerodynamic models used in flight-dynamics applications.

The training is structured around the key technical areas reflected in MIT AeroAstro's Flight Vehicle Aerodynamics subject, including subsonic, transonic and supersonic aerodynamic flows.

Professional Learning Objectives

  • Understand aerodynamic flow modelling and representation techniques.
  • Analyse airfoil and lifting-surface aerodynamic behaviour.
  • Understand laminar and turbulent boundary-layer effects.
  • Analyse aerodynamic forces in nearfield and farfield conditions.
  • Understand subsonic, transonic and supersonic compressible flows.
  • Apply experimental methods and aerodynamic measurement techniques.
  • Understand aerodynamic models used in flight-dynamics analysis.

Course Programme

Day 1 — Aerodynamic Flow Modelling

  • Fundamentals of aerodynamic flow modelling
  • Flow representation techniques
  • Potential-flow concepts
  • Airfoil aerodynamic behaviour
  • Lifting-surface theory
  • Aerodynamic performance fundamentals

Day 2 — Boundary Layers & Viscous Aerodynamics

  • Laminar boundary layers
  • Turbulent boundary layers
  • Boundary-layer effects on aerodynamic performance
  • Flow separation and aerodynamic losses
  • Nearfield and farfield aerodynamic behaviour
  • Aerodynamic force analysis

Day 3 — Compressible Aerodynamics

  • Fundamentals of compressible aerodynamic flows
  • Subsonic aerodynamic flows
  • Transonic flow behaviour
  • Supersonic aerodynamic flows
  • Shock and expansion phenomena
  • Compressible-flow performance considerations

Day 4 — Experimental Aerodynamics & Measurement

  • Experimental aerodynamic methods
  • Aerodynamic measurement techniques
  • Wind-tunnel testing concepts
  • Aerodynamic data interpretation
  • Validation of aerodynamic models
  • Engineering application of experimental results

Day 5 — Flight Vehicle Aerodynamic Applications

  • Aerodynamic models for flight dynamics
  • Aircraft performance applications
  • Aerodynamic design considerations
  • Integration of aerodynamic data into vehicle analysis
  • Engineering case studies
  • Final technical discussion and review

Flight Vehicle Aerodynamics in Practice

The programme connects aerodynamic theory with practical flight-vehicle engineering, helping participants understand how aerodynamic behaviour influences aircraft performance, analysis and design decisions.
