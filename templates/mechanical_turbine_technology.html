Course Description

Advanced Mechanical Engineering with Power Plant Technologies provides mechanical engineers with an in-depth technical understanding of advanced mechanical engineering topics relevant to the power generation industry.

Participants develop practical understanding of state-of-the-art techniques used in gas and wind turbine control systems and learn the principles required to make sound engineering judgements during system design, deployment, operation and maintenance.

Course Code LONG 5
Duration 5 Days
Course Date Specific date request
Venue Marriott Islamabad

Course Objectives

  • Explain the main components, mechanical systems and basic operation of wind and gas turbines.
  • Test electrical components, mechanical and hydraulic systems to troubleshoot malfunctions.
  • Maintain and repair central systems and components.
  • Replace worn or malfunctioning components, monitor stock and order spare parts.
  • Explain risks and hazards associated with hydraulic work.

  • Mechanical Engineers
