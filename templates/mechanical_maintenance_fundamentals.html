Course Description

This course provides a solid foundation in the essential principles, practices, and tools of mechanical maintenance. Designed to enhance both technical knowledge and practical skills, it covers the maintenance of mechanical equipment.

The course also introduces maintenance planning and documentation practices critical to effective asset management. By the end of this course, participants will apply newly gained knowledge of mechanical systems as they learn to use industrial precision measuring instruments to perform a variety of maintenance tasks.

Course Objectives

Understanding the proper selection and use of common hand and power tools
Learning the ability to select, use and read precision measurement tools
Familiarizing with common rigging and lifting equipment, and techniques
Learning how to read mechanical prints
Making fundamental power systems calculations

Who Should Attend?

  • Mechanical Fitters
  • Mechanical and Plant Engineers
  • Supervisors and Maintenance Managers

Course Syllabus

Day 1 — Maintenance Fundamentals and Hand Tools

  • Impact of maintenance
  • Fundamental requirements of effective maintenance
  • Planning and scheduling
  • Maintenance engineering roles and responsibilities
  • Selection and use of hand tools

Day 2 — Technical Calculations and Measurements

  • Technical calculations and measurements
  • Mathematical applications in technical environments
  • Fundamental conversion methodology and calculations
  • Major systems of measurements
  • Measuring instruments
  • Measurement error and tolerance
  • Data tables

Day 3 — Mechanical Print Reading

  • Mechanical print reading
  • Different lines and symbols
  • Visible and hidden lines
  • Sectional views and dimensions
  • Isometric drawings
  • Orthographic projections and written information

Day 4 — Fluid Power, Power Systems and Rigging

  • Fluid power systems
  • Basic fluid and pneumatic terminology
  • Fundamental power systems calculations
  • Typical power system applications
  • Rigging and lifting

Day 5 — Bearings, Lubrication and Servicing

  • Bearings and lubrication
  • Procedures for servicing
  • Inspecting
  • Disassembling
  • Reassembling plain journal bearings
  • Anti-friction journal bearings
  • Thrust bearings
