Aircraft Structural Integrity Programme

CC’s professional aerospace engineering training develops practical expertise in aircraft structural integrity, fatigue, damage tolerance, inspection, assessment and lifecycle management.

Course Reference ASI 201
Duration 5 Days
Course Date Can be offered upon a specific date request
Venue Marriott Islamabad

Course Description

This professional programme provides a structured understanding of aircraft structural integrity and the engineering principles used to maintain the safety, reliability and service life of aircraft structures.

Participants explore fatigue behaviour, damage tolerance, fracture mechanics, structural inspection, non-destructive evaluation, structural assessment and lifecycle management of aircraft structures.

Course Objectives

  • Understand the fundamental principles of aircraft structural integrity.
  • Understand fatigue, fracture and damage-tolerance concepts.
  • Assess structural degradation and potential failure mechanisms.
  • Understand inspection and structural monitoring approaches.
  • Apply engineering principles to structural life assessment.
  • Understand the relationship between structural integrity and continued aircraft safety.
  • Develop practical knowledge of aircraft structural lifecycle management.

Who Should Attend

  • Aerospace Engineers
  • Aircraft Structural Engineers
  • Maintenance Engineers
  • Aircraft Inspectors
  • Materials and Metallurgical Engineers
  • Quality and Reliability Professionals
  • Engineering Managers and Technical Professionals

Five-Day Training Programme

Day 1 — Aircraft Structural Integrity Fundamentals

  • Introduction to aircraft structural integrity
  • Aircraft structural configurations
  • Structural loads and operational environments
  • Material behaviour and structural performance
  • Structural safety and lifecycle concepts

Day 2 — Fatigue & Fracture Mechanics

  • Aircraft structural fatigue
  • Cyclic loading and fatigue damage
  • Crack initiation and propagation
  • Fracture mechanics fundamentals
  • Fatigue-life assessment

Day 3 — Damage Tolerance & Structural Assessment

  • Damage-tolerance principles
  • Fail-safe structural concepts
  • Crack growth assessment
  • Residual strength
  • Structural failure assessment

Day 4 — Inspection & Structural Health

  • Aircraft structural inspection principles
  • Non-destructive inspection methods
  • Damage detection and characterization
  • Structural health monitoring
  • Inspection planning and assessment

Day 5 — Lifecycle Management & Engineering Applications

  • Structural lifecycle management
  • Continued structural integrity
  • Maintenance programme considerations
  • Structural modification and repair assessment
  • Engineering case studies and practical applications
