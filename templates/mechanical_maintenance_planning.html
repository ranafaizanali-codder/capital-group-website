Professional Training Program

Planning and Programming of Mechanical Maintenance

A professional maintenance training program focused on maintenance planning, reliability, equipment replacement, inspection intervals, plant shutdowns, turnaround planning and computerized maintenance management systems.

Important Note: The minimum number of participants to conduct this course is 3 persons.

Maintenance Planning

Develop structured maintenance planning methods and integrated organizational approaches.

Reliability & Optimization

Understand reliability, dependability, maintenance modeling and optimization techniques.

Maintenance Programming

Improve shutdown planning, equipment replacement, inspection intervals and maintenance reporting.

Course Description

Make safety a number one priority when establishing maintenance procedures. Managers should ensure that employees always have proper equipment, ventilation and lighting, and that all equipment is code compliant.

Having an effective mechanical maintenance program in place will assist in making the workplace safe and assure staff of steady, uninterrupted employment.

Course Objectives

  • Creating awareness of planning methods and an integrated organizational approach.
  • Learning the theories, methods and tools for better understanding of reliability and dependability.
  • Understanding maintenance modeling and optimization.
  • Understanding replacement decisions.
  • Learning how to develop optimum inspection intervals for individual equipment.
  • Ensuring a team approach in the planning and execution of plant shutdowns and turnarounds.
  • Making technological design of facilities for maintenance.

Who Should Attend?

  • Operations supervisors
  • Maintenance, engineering and technical support staff
  • Leadership and management, planners, maintenance supervisors and engineers
