Professional Training Program

EEE 189 - Power Electronics Rectifier & Inverter

This course on Power Electronics Rectifiers and Inverters covers the fundamental principles, operation, and design of rectifiers and inverters used in power electronics applications. The course is designed for participants with a background in electrical engineering or a related field.

Participants will develop a strong understanding of power electronic devices, single-phase and three-phase rectifiers, inverters and their applications in various industries.

Learning Outcomes

Course Objectives

  • Introducing the fundamental principles and operation of power electronics rectifiers and inverters.
  • Providing an understanding of the different types of power electronic devices used in rectifiers and inverters.
  • Learning about single-phase and three-phase rectifiers, half-wave and full-wave rectifiers, bridge rectifiers, filter circuits, and voltage regulation.
  • Discussing different types of inverters.
  • Understanding the applications of power electronics in various industries.
Target Participants

Who Should Attend?

  • Electronics engineers
  • Power Systems Engineers
  • Individuals working in renewable energy, motor drives, electric vehicles, power supplies, and lighting systems
