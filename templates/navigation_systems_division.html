NAVIGATION SYSTEMS DIVISION

Precision Manufacturing Solutions par Excellence

CCs Navigation Systems Division specializes in precision-engineered aerospace navigation hardware, inertial navigation systems, GPS and GNSS receivers, heading systems and navigation display technologies for advanced aircraft applications.

OUR CAPABILITIES

Aerospace Navigation Systems

CECs provides precision-engineered navigation technologies integrating inertial sensors, satellite navigation receivers, heading systems, navigation computers and aircraft display interfaces.

01

Inertial Navigation Systems

Precision inertial navigation units combine gyroscopes, accelerometers and navigation electronics to provide continuous aircraft position, attitude and motion information.

02

GPS & GNSS Receivers

Advanced satellite-navigation receivers provide accurate positioning and timing information for aerospace platforms operating with GPS, GNSS and integrated navigation architectures.

03

Multi-Mode Navigation Receivers

Integrated receiver hardware combines multiple navigation and landing signals into dependable navigation solutions for demanding commercial and aerospace applications.

04

Gyro & Heading Systems

Precision gyro and heading components provide reliable directional reference information for aircraft navigation, attitude determination and integrated avionics systems.

05

Navigation Displays & Interfaces

Navigation display interfaces present aircraft heading, route, position and navigation data through clear electronic cockpit information systems for operational awareness and control.
