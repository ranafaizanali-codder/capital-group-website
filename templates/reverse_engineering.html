REVERSE ENGINEERING CONSULTANCY

Turning Existing Products into Engineering Knowledge

Reverse engineering combines technical inspection, measurement, analysis and engineering interpretation to understand how an existing product or component is designed and manufactured. A structured approach can support redesign, modernization, replacement development and product improvement.

Product and Component Assessment

A detailed assessment establishes the physical and functional characteristics of an existing product. Understanding dimensions, interfaces, materials and operating requirements provides a foundation for further engineering analysis.

Dimensional Assessment Evaluation of physical dimensions, interfaces and component geometry.
Component Identification Structured identification and classification of product components and assemblies.
Functional Analysis Examination of component functions and relationships within the overall system.

Engineering Data Reconstruction

Existing physical products can be translated into structured engineering information. Technical reconstruction supports the development of drawings, specifications and digital engineering references for further design work.

Technical Drawings Development of engineering drawing information from assessed components and assemblies.
Geometry Reconstruction Reconstruction of component geometry and relevant dimensional relationships.
Engineering Documentation Organization of technical information for future engineering and manufacturing activities.

Materials and Manufacturing Analysis

Understanding materials and manufacturing methods can provide important insight into product performance and production requirements. Technical assessment supports informed decisions for redesign, replacement and manufacturing development.

Material Assessment Technical consideration of material characteristics relevant to product functionality.
Manufacturing Process Review Analysis of possible manufacturing approaches and production characteristics.
Design for Manufacturing Consideration of practical manufacturing requirements during engineering development.

Redesign, Modernization and Product Development

Reverse engineering can provide a technical foundation for improving an existing product. Reconstructed engineering knowledge can support redesign, modernization, component replacement and development of updated solutions.

Design Improvement Identification of opportunities for technical improvement and product development.
Component Replacement Engineering support for developing replacement components and compatible alternatives.
Product Modernization Application of reconstructed engineering information to modernization initiatives.
