Mandarin Division

EXPORT, IMPORT AND GLOBAL SOURCING / SUPPLY CHAIN SOLUTIONS.

CTC’s Mandarin Division specializes in sourcing, grading, packing and supplying premium Pakistani mandarins for domestic and international markets, with a focus on quality, freshness and dependable export logistics.

Premium Pakistani Mandarins

CTC’s Mandarin Division connects Pakistan’s major citrus growing regions with international buyers through carefully selected fruit, professional grading, export-oriented packing and reliable supply-chain management.

VARIETY 01

Kinnow Mandarin

Pakistan’s principal commercial mandarin, valued for its strong juice content, vibrant orange colour, easy-peel skin and balanced sweet-tangy flavour. It is widely grown in the Sargodha citrus belt and is the country's leading citrus export crop.

Quality Deep orange, juicy, aromatic, export-graded
Availability December – March
Quantity Commercial bulk supply; export cartons / reefer loads
VARIETY 02

Feutrell's Early

An early-maturing Pakistani mandarin known for its juicy and sweet fruit, smooth skin and easy peeling. Its earlier harvest window helps extend the citrus supply season before the main Kinnow crop.

Quality Juicy, sweet, smooth-skinned and easy to peel
Availability October – November
Quantity 86–114 kg/tree average; 600–800 fruits/tree
VARIETY 03

AARI Seedless Kinnow

A Pakistani seedless Kinnow selection developed to address growing demand for easier-to-eat citrus. Its seedless character gives it strong potential for premium fresh-fruit markets and modern export supply chains.

Quality Seedless character, juicy premium fruit
Availability January
Quantity Commercial cultivation being expanded
VARIETY 04

Daisy Mandarin

A mid-season mandarin with attractive dark-orange colour, juicy flesh and a distinctive citrus profile. Daisy has also been commercialized in Pakistan as part of efforts to diversify the country's citrus production.

Quality Medium-large fruit, juicy and attractive colour
Availability Mid-November – December
Quantity Approx. 80–120 kg/tree under reported conditions
