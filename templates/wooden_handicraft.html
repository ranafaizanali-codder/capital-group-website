CC’s Wooden Handicraft Division connects traditional Chinioti craftsmanship with modern markets through carefully selected carved wood products and furniture.

Chinioti Woodcraft — Crafted by Heritage

Chiniot is renowned for its traditional wood carving and handcrafted furniture. Skilled artisans create detailed designs in solid wood, combining traditional craftsmanship with refined finishing for distinctive domestic and international markets.

Traditional Craft

Intricate Hand Carving

Traditional Chinioti artisans transform solid wood into detailed floral and geometric designs using skilled hand-carving techniques passed through generations.

Premium Material

Solid Sheesham Wood

Sheesham is widely associated with Chinioti furniture, valued for its strength, natural grain and suitability for detailed carving and long-lasting handcrafted work.

Signature Detail

Carving & Brass Inlay

Selected Chinioti pieces combine deep wood carving with refined brass inlay, creating distinctive decorative surfaces with a rich traditional character.

Crafted Collections

Furniture & Decorative Pieces

The craft extends beyond furniture to decorative wooden pieces, mirror frames, cabinets, table sets and other handcrafted products suitable for residential and commercial markets.
