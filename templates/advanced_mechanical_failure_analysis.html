MMS 116 - Advanced Topics in Mechanical Failures and System Analysis

Mechanical failures are the cause of the production stops and should be eliminated with correct maintenance and understanding of failures. Mechanical failure analysis provides the causes of the failures, which ought to be corrected for efficient production.

This course discusses schematic design, system analysis, construction documentation and design visualization as well as learning the causes of mechanical fail and the methods for analysing and predicting the structural integrity of a mechanical machines which is extremely important in industry.

Course Duration: 5 Days
Course Date: Can be offered upon a specific date request
Venue: Marriott Islamabad

Course Objectives

  • Understanding mechanical systems
  • Familiarizing with mechanical failure analysis
  • Being prepared to address reliability related to mechanical equipment
  • Learning failure modes and designing fail-safe systems
  • Understanding the service and maintenance requirements for machine failures
  • Providing competency in the predominant tools of mechanical system

Who Should Attend?

  • Those who are beginning their reliability career
  • Reliability specialists
  • Designers
  • Engineers
  • Managers

5-Day Course Syllabus

Day 1 — Mechanical Systems and Failure Fundamentals

  • Overview of mechanical systems
  • Schematic design
  • System analysis
  • What is mechanical failure
  • Failure Modes
  • Cause of failures in production
  • Effective ways to prevent failure or live with the failures

Day 2 — Static Failure Modes and Strength

  • Static failure modes
  • Fracture
  • Buckling
  • Impact
  • Strength calculations
  • Material and dimension selection

Day 3 — Dynamic Failure Modes

  • Dynamic failure modes
  • Fatigue
  • Creep
  • Wear
  • Corrosion

Day 4 — Crack Growth and Failure Testing

  • Crack growth
  • Failure rate
  • Hazard analysis
  • Impact testing
  • Fatigue testing

Day 5 — Failure Improvement and Engineering Applications

  • Failure testing
  • Improving fatigue life
  • Improving creep life
  • Structural examples
  • Bearing example
  • Construction documentation
  • Design visualization
