Professional Training Program

MMS 117 - Measuring Mechanical Vibrations and Methods of Measurement

This course provides participants with fundamental knowledge of mechanical vibration measurement, harmonic motion, mechanical system modelling, vibration response, measurement instruments and experimental modal analysis.

Vibration Fundamentals

Understand mechanical vibration, harmonic motion, natural frequencies and system response.

Measurement Systems

Develop practical knowledge of vibration sensors, transducers and measurement techniques.

Condition Monitoring

Apply vibration analysis concepts to machine condition monitoring and vibration suppression.

Course Description

This course is designed to provide participants with an introduction to the fundamental concepts of vibration measurement. Participants will learn an appreciation for harmonic motion as well as the modeling of mechanical systems. The course also covers free and forced vibration of single-degree-of-freedom and multi-degree-of-freedom systems, modal superposition, vibration isolation and damping, vibration measuring instruments, discrete and continuous systems and an introduction to experimental modal analysis.

Course Objectives

Familiarizing with the concepts of mechanical vibrations
Understanding vibration measurement systems
Being able to interpret mechanical vibrations test requirements
Studying the response of discrete and distributed systems to various inputs
Learning how to design systems for vibration suppression and machine condition monitoring
