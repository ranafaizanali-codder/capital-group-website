Our Capabilities

CNC Machines & Specialized Machining

An overview of the machining processes and facilities provided for engineering and industrial applications.

01 / TURNING

CNC Lathe Machines

CNC turning for cylindrical components, shafts, rings and other rotationally machined parts across a range of chuck sizes and bed lengths.

Stated range: 1-inch to 1-meter diameter; lengths from 6 inches to 20 feet.
02 / MILLING

CNC High-Speed Milling

High-speed milling for detailed features, precision surfaces and components requiring efficient material removal.

03 / MULTI-AXIS

3-Axis to 5-Axis CNC Milling

Multi-axis milling and machining centers for complex geometries, multiple-face machining and components with demanding dimensional requirements.

Stated job size: 6 inches to 1 meter.
04 / LARGE FORMAT

Moving-Column CNC Milling

Large-format milling for extended components and machining applications requiring a long working bed.

Stated bed length: 14 meters.
05 / ROLLING

CNC Rolling

Rolling capability for forming material to the required profile, radius or curvature, subject to material and job specifications.

Stated material thickness range: 1 mm to 40 mm.
06 / DRILLING

CNC Precision Drilling

Controlled drilling for accurately positioned holes and repeatable hole-making operations in engineering components.

Stated drilling range: 1 mm to 100 mm.
07 / MULTI-HEAD

CNC Multi-Head Drills

Multi-head drilling capability for coordinated hole-making operations where component layout and production requirements are suitable.

08 / BORE MACHINING

CNC Jig Boring

Precision boring for accurately located holes, bore finishing and components requiring close positional control.

09 / GRINDING

CNC Multi-Grinding

Grinding processes for surface finish, dimensional control and component finishing across suitable geometries.

Processes include surface, cylindrical and centerless grinding.
10 / SHEET PROCESSING

CNC Nibbling & Punching

Sheet-metal nibbling and punching for profiles, openings and repeated features according to component drawings.

11 / TUBE FORMING

CNC Tube Bending

Controlled tube forming for specified bends and component shapes, subject to tube material, wall thickness and bend-radius requirements.

Stated tube size range: 3 mm to 10 mm.
12 / WIRE CUTTING

CNC Wire-Cut Machines

Wire-cut machining for intricate profiles, precision contours and features that are difficult to produce with conventional cutting methods.

Workpiece capacity depends on machine and setup.
13 / EDM

Electrical Discharge Machining (EDM)

Electrical discharge machining for suitable electrically conductive materials and detailed features requiring controlled material removal.

14 / PLANING

CNC Planer

Planing operations for machining broad surfaces and selected large components, depending on workpiece geometry and machine capacity.

15 / SHAPING

CNC Shaper

Shaping operations for suitable flat surfaces, slots and component features based on the required geometry.

16 / GEAR MACHINING

CNC Hobbing Machines

Gear hobbing for manufacturing compatible gear teeth and related components according to specified gear geometry.

17 / FINISHING

CNC Honing

Honing for controlled internal surface finishing and bore geometry where the component and tolerance requirements are suitable.

18 / MATERIAL REMOVAL

Gouging

Gouging capability for designated material-removal and component preparation applications, based on the required process and material.
