ISO 14001 Environmental Management System (EMS) Lead Auditor

An Environmental Management System (EMS) is a set of processes and practices that enable an organization to reduce its environmental impacts and increase its operating efficiency. This highly interactive and practical course is designed to provide delegates with a sound knowledge of all elements involved in implementing an Environmental Management System (EMS) using the standards.

Course Code ISO 102
Duration 5 Days
Course Date Can be offered against a specific date request
Venue Marriott Islamabad

Course Objectives

  • Identifying the objectives and benefits of an EMS and the requirements of the standard.
  • Learning how to assess an organization's current state with regards to an EMS.
  • Being familiar with performing a gap analysis to meet EMS standards.
  • Planning, developing and implementing an EMS appropriate to your organization.
  • Identifying environmental aspects and impacts relevant to your organization with the emphasis on improvement.

Who Should Attend?

  • Health & Safety managers
  • Supervisors
  • Executives
  • Auditors
  • Anyone involved in implementing, maintaining and improving an EMS
Course Syllabus

Five-Day Professional Training Program

Day 1 — Environmental Management System Foundations

  • Introduction to Environmental Management System (EMS)
  • Review of environmental legislation
  • The audit process
  • Codes of EMS practice
  • The benefits of an EMS based on ISO 14001
  • Identification of environmental aspects and impacts
  • Risk evaluation

Day 2 — ISO 14001 Requirements and Audit Planning

  • Defining an environmental policy
  • Establishing policy and setting objectives and targets
  • The principles underpinning the ISO 14001 standard
  • ISO 14001 standards for commitments to pollution prevention
  • Planning audits and reviewing documentation
  • Audit reports and corrective action

Day 3 — EMS Implementation and Performance

  • Planning the EMS implementation
  • Timescales and resources
  • Gaining management commitment
  • Environmental performance measures
  • Monitoring and auditing for improvement

Day 4 — Environmental Auditing and Management Systems

  • Process based auditing and sampling
  • Integrating ISO 14001 with other management systems
  • Policy, objectives & targets development
  • Environmental management program
  • The issues related to effective implementation of an environmental management system
  • How to manage an audit program

Day 5 — Compliance, Certification and Continual Improvement

  • Continual improvement and legal and other compliance
  • Accreditation and certification activities
  • Sources of information and further development
  • Legal/ethical aspects of auditing
  • General review of the course

Develop practical knowledge of ISO 14001, environmental auditing, compliance and Environmental Management System implementation.
