Introduction to Satellite Communications & Marine VSAT

CC’s professional marine engineering training develops practical expertise in satellite communication, digital communications, marine VSAT systems, stabilized antennas and maritime connectivity.

TCM 140 - Introduction to Satellite Communications & Marine VSAT

Course Reference TCM 140
Duration 5 Days
Venue Marriott Islamabad

Course Description

This training course will introduce the basics of digital communication and VSAT systems, explaining the particularities of Marine VSAT Systems and Stabilized Antennas. It will focus in operation of Marine VSAT systems with hands-on labs and exercises and in troubleshooting using real Marine VSAT antennas and equipment. The use of the Spectrum Analyzer will be shown for a better faultfinding.

Course Objectives

  • Describe VSAT service characteristics and manage the service using Custom Support Portal.
  • Describe and compare the parameters that characterize a VSAT service.
  • Predict the operation of a marine VSAT system under normal conditions.
  • Identify the hazards involved when working with marine VSAT systems.
  • Perform a basic site survey and evaluate existing installations conditions.
  • Configure and operate marine VSAT systems.

Who should Attend?

  • Practising engineers
  • Information technologists
  • Operation staff
  • Anyone who needs a technical introduction to SATCOM and VSAT
