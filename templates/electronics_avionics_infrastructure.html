Capital Engineering Corporation provides a broad range of electronics production and laboratory capabilities, combining multilayer PCB manufacturing, advanced component assembly, and electronic measurement equipment. Our infrastructure supports work across circuit fabrication, component integration, signal evaluation, and electrical testing, with processes selected to meet each project's technical requirements and quality objectives.

Manufacturing & Testing Capabilities

Six integrated capability areas covering circuit boards, assembly processes, and electronic test instrumentation.

PCB Manufacturing

Multilayer printed circuit board manufacturing for complex electronic designs and demanding applications.

  • PCB manufacturing capability up to 40 copper layers
  • Copper, silver and gold finishes, subject to process availability
  • Design and quality requirements aligned with applicable AS9100 and IPC Class 3 requirements, where specified

PCB Assembly & Component Integration

Assembly options for through-hole and surface-mount components across a range of electronic board designs.

  • Manual PCB assembly line
  • Through-Hole Technology (THT) assembly line
  • Wave-soldering process for compatible through-hole assemblies
  • Surface-Mount Technology (SMT) assembly line

High-Speed Pick-and-Place

Automated component placement for fine-pitch PCB assembly, supporting a variety of integrated circuit packages and surface-mount devices.

  • Specified placement range: 0.18–0.25 mm, subject to component and process requirements
  • DIP, QFP, BGA and SMT component technologies, where supported by the equipment
  • Placement of a range of IC packages and electronic devices
